Frequently Asked Questions

How long does token deployment take?

Token deployment typically takes 1-3 minutes after you confirm the transaction in your wallet. The exact time depends on Ethereum network congestion. You can track your deployment progress in the dashboard.

Why is my transaction taking so long?

Transaction times depend on the gas fee you paid and current network congestion. You can speed up a pending transaction by increasing the gas fee in your wallet. If a transaction is stuck for more than 24 hours, it will eventually be dropped by the network.

How do I connect my wallet to Ethora?

Click the "Connect Wallet" button in the top right corner of the dashboard. Select your wallet provider (MetaMask, WalletConnect, etc.) and authorize the connection. Make sure you're on the Ethereum Mainnet.

What are the gas fee requirements?

Gas fees vary based on Ethereum network congestion. Token deployment typically requires 0.01-0.05 ETH in gas fees. We recommend checking current gas prices on Etherscan before initiating transactions. During high congestion periods, consider waiting or using our "Low Gas" option.

Can I change token parameters after deployment?

Most token parameters (name, symbol, total supply) are immutable after deployment. Some parameters like taxes and wallet limits can be changed if you deployed our advanced token contract. Refer to your token's contract documentation for specific capabilities.

How do I verify my token contract?

After deployment, go to the "Token Management" section in your dashboard. Click "Verify Contract" and follow the steps to submit your source code to Etherscan. Our system automatically prepares the verification files for you.
